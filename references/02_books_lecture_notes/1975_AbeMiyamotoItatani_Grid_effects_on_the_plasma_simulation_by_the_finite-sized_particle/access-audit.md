## Bounded abstract evidence

The abstract supports these narrow claims:

- ordinary momentum-conserving finite-grid models do not conserve total energy exactly;
- grid-induced kinetic-energy fluctuation has a stochastic origin;
- the paper studies the standard deviation `sigma(K_g)` and correlation time of the energy increment;
- a one-dimensional periodic system is used to obtain a scaling law involving grid count, density, Debye length, plasma frequency and correlation time;
- CIC-PIC, modified SUDS and method 2/2 are reported as numerical checks of the scaling.

The abstract does not support a full derivation, complete case table, or a line-by-line comparison with the current WarpX kernels. The formula text exposed by the index is kept as a source cue, not treated as a fully normalized transcription.
